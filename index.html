About

I am an astrophysics graduate student at the University of Sheffield. My work combines observational and computational approaches to investigate ultracool dwarf atmospheres, exploring the potential relationships between auroral activity, atmospheric chemistry and spectroscopic variability.

Research

Current Projects

  • Investigating the variability of LSR J1835+3259 using JWST/NIRISS-SOSS time-series spectroscopy.
  • Using chemical network models to investigate the impact of auroral electron beams on ultracool dwarf atmospheres.

Supervisor: Prof. S Littlefair · Astrophysics Research Cluster

Publications

  • E. Pike et al. (2026). CJWST/NIRISS-SOSS Spectroscopic Variability and Auroral Modelling of Warm Ultracool Dwarf LSR J1835+3259. MNRAS (under review).
  • E. Pike et al. The Chemical Impact of Auroral Electron Beams on Ultracool Dwarf Atmospheres. (in preparation).
