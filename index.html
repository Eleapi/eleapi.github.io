About

I am an astrophysics graduate student at the University of Sheffield. My work combines observational and computational approaches to investigate ultracool dwarf atmospheres, exploring the potential relationships between auroral activity, atmospheric chemistry and spectroscopic variability.

Research

Current Projects

  • JWST/NIRISS-SOSS spectroscopic variability of LSR J1835+3259.
  • Using chemical network models to investigate the impact of auroral electron beams on ultracool dwarf atmospheres.
