About

I am an astrophysics PhD student at the University of Sheffield. My work combines observational and computational approaches to investigate ultracool dwarf atmospheres, exploring the potential relationships between auroral activity, atmospheric chemistry and spectroscopic variability.

Research

JWST Spectroscopy and Atmospheric Variability

I use time-resolved JWST/NIRISS spectroscopy of auroral ultracool dwarf LSR J1835+3259 to investigate how its infrared spectrum changes over time, alongside atmospheric modelling to explore the physical processes responsible for that variability.

Auroral Chemistry in Ultracool Dwarf Atmospheres

I use the STAND/ARGO chemical kinetics framework to investigate how auroral electron precipitation affects the chemical composition of ultracool dwarf atmospheres, and what this might mean for their observable spectra.

Supervisor: Prof. S. Littlefair · Astrophysics Research Cluster, University of Sheffield
